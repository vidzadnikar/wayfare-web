    Claude is built in.

    Open the Claude panel next to the timeline, connect your Claude subscription (through Claude Code on your computer) or an Anthropic API key, and say what you want. Claude edits with the app's own tools; you see every step and undo it with ⌘Z.

    Already on Claude Code? Our vlog skill works there too:

    # in Claude Code, no Node.js needed:

    /plugin marketplace add vidzadnikar/wayfare-skills

    /plugin install wayfare@wayfare

    # then:

    Make a vlog from my Lisbon clips.

    npx skills add vidzadnikar/wayfare-skills --skill vlog -g -a claude-code

    # then, in Claude Code:

    Make a vlog from my Lisbon clips.

    Needs Claude Code. The npx route also needs Node.js.

    Common questions.

    Is Wayfare really free?

    The app is: every feature is free, with no subscription and no limits. The automatic edit is done by Claude, which needs your own Claude subscription (Pro or Max) or an Anthropic API key, where you pay only for what you use. Without Claude, you edit on the timeline yourself.

    Where do my clips go?

    They stay on your computer. Nothing is uploaded until you publish.

    Do I need to know how to edit?

    No. Claude turns the day into a story, and you say what you want changed in one sentence. If you like, you can also edit everything yourself on the timeline.

    What do I need for Claude?

    A Claude subscription (Pro or Max) with Claude Code on your computer: the panel opens Anthropic's installer and you sign in with Anthropic. Or an Anthropic API key, where you pay only for what you use.

    Which devices does it run on?

    Macs with Apple silicon (macOS 12 or later), 64-bit Windows 10 or later, and Android 8 or later. There is no Linux version yet.

    Why does my Mac warn me the first time?

    The app isn’t notarized with Apple yet. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, click “Open Anyway” at the bottom and confirm. From then on it opens normally.

    How do I tell you what to fix?

    Use the feedback form, or in the app open the ⋯ menu and choose Send feedback. Every message goes to the person who builds Wayfare Studio.

    Install with a command (advanced)

    If you know Terminal or PowerShell, you can install Wayfare Studio with one command. It downloads the same package from GitHub, checks its checksum and installs the app.

    Mac, in Terminal:

    curl -fsSL https://wayfarestudio.pages.dev/install.sh | bash

    Windows, in PowerShell:

    irm https://wayfarestudio.pages.dev/install.ps1 | iex

    Android, with your phone:

    Scan the code with your phone’s camera and open the link: the app downloads straight to the phone. Android asks once whether to allow installing from your browser.

    Add to Obtainium: installs Wayfare from GitHub and keeps it up to date by itself (install Obtainium first).
