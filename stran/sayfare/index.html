Three steps. No typing.

  1. ⌥Space

    Press the shortcut

    ⌥ Space on a Mac, Alt + Space on Windows: works in every app, and you can pick your own. On Android you dictate with the Sayfare keyboard.

  2. Talk

    Like you would to a friend. Full stops and commas go in by themselves.

  3. Aa

    Press it again

    The text goes where your cursor is. While you talk, you only see a small pill.

Made for Slovenian. And fast.

  • Understands Slovenian

    Whisper large-v3-turbo transcribes your voice and knows the cases, the dual and names. Add words you often say to the dictionary.

  • Types while you talk

    Sayfare transcribes as you speak. When you stop, the text is there in a second or two, even after a minute of talking.

  • A proofreader with Claude

    If you have Claude Code or a Claude API key, a proofreader reads the text after you: commas, capitals, typos. Without it you get a clean transcript.

  • Stays on your computer

    On Mac and Windows a model on your computer transcribes your voice. Recordings delete themselves after two hours.

Get Sayfare.

Version 0.1.0 is free. On first launch your computer also downloads the transcription model: 1.6 GB on a Mac, 0.6 GB on Windows.

Installing on a Mac

  1. Open the downloaded file and drag Sayfare into Applications.
  2. First time: open the app. The Mac stops it because it isn’t notarized with Apple yet. In System Settings → Privacy & Security, click “Open Anyway” and confirm.
  3. Allow the microphone and Accessibility. Without Accessibility the text stays on the clipboard, and you paste it with ⌘V.

If the Mac says the app is damaged, run this in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Sayfare.app

Installing on Windows

  1. Open the downloaded installer. It isn’t signed yet, so Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”: click More info, then Run anyway.
  2. Sayfare installs without admin rights and downloads the transcription model on first launch. Then press Alt + Space in any app.

Installing on Android

  1. Open the downloaded file and allow installing apps from your browser.
  2. In Sayfare › Settings, turn on the Sayfare keyboard and add the tile to Quick Settings. Google speech recognition transcribes; with an ElevenLabs key the transcript is more accurate.
